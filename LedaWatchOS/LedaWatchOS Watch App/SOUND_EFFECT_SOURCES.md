# Sound effect direction

Zedge was reviewed for short Ben 10 / Omnitrix cues:

- [Ben 10 watch](https://www.zedge.net/notification-sounds/f2585068-ac20-4717-9ea0-35a5758d4f18) — activation cue
- [Omnitrix](https://www.zedge.net/notification-sounds/683d00d1-06ee-4694-b61b-eaacaa260600) — short Crown/dial cue
- [Omnitrix clean sound](https://www.zedge.net/notification-sounds/26fe0c33-3db9-4705-ae9b-91ee53a67d66) — selector cue
- [Ben 10 Omniverse Omnitrix Alien Confirm](https://www.zedge.net/notification-sounds/b80ee100-79a3-478d-9a6a-4159894934f5) — alien selection confirmation
- [Ben 10](https://www.zedge.net/notification-sounds/ede7f2d2-d9e5-4665-ade9-75a9fb1317e6) — short status cue

The bundled Ben 10 clips are used directly for the interaction cues. The older synthesized files remain in the folder only as unused fallback files and are no longer referenced by the app.
